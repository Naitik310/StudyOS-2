About StudyOS 2

A calmer way to organise your academic life.

StudyOS 2

Your study workspace, built around your workflow.

Class 11

StudyOS 2 brings syllabus management, study planning, daily tasks, progress tracking, revision and academic quick references together in one place. Its goal is to make a busy academic workload easier to understand and manage.

A note from the creator

StudyOS 2 started with a simple thought: studying shouldn't feel like trying to manage a hundred things at once. I wanted to build something that helps students organise their workload, understand their progress, and make study days a little less overwhelming.

This is a personal project, built with the hope that it makes someone's academic journey easier. It's still growing, and your feedback can help make it better.

Thanks for being part of the journey.
